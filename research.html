Research Projects

Boltzmann Transport Solver for 2D Materials Repository

C++ High-Performance Computing OpenMP / MPI

A highly parallelized C++ package designed to simulate and study quantum effects in materials. This solver models the Boltzmann Transport Equation (BTE) to predict electrical properties, charge transport dynamics, and evaluate strategies to improve the thermoelectric efficiency of semiconductor structures. The tool handles complex scattering mechanisms and outputs data for further analysis.

Simulating Quantum Effects in Semiconductor Nanostructures

MATLAB Python Predictive Modeling

Predictive modeling suite built to understand charge transport dynamics across multi-layered and twisted heterostructures (such as twisted bilayer graphene). Prototypes were originally modeled in MATLAB and scaled using high-performance cloud environments to handle massive probabilistic system simulations for energy harvesting scenarios.
