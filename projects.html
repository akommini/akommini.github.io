Research Projects

  • High throughput 2D material discovery for thermoelectric applications: predicting power factor using physics aware models
    Calculates the power factor of 2D materials using existing databases by accurately modeling carrier transport.
  • Phonon drag contribution towards TE performance in MoS2: Using full phonon distribution
    Studies the contribution of different phonon modes towards the drag by calculating the full phonon distribution including normal processes.
  • Anisotropy in power factor of 2D materials with periodic potential barriers using Wigner-Rode formalism
    Evaluates the anisotropy in power factor that arises from carriers being transported by different processes in 2D materials. Journal Paper
  • Towards a higher power factor in 2D materials: Role of inelastic scattering mechanisms
    Determines optimal material parameters to optimize the thermoelectric power factor using a novel controlled-scattering approach. Journal Paper
  • Thermoelectric properties of periodic quantum structures in the Wigner-Rode formalism
    Implements a comprehensive transport model using the Wigner–Rode formalism to study tunneling and barrier impacts in semiconducting nanostructures. Journal Paper
  • Fowler-Nordheim emission in surface modified free-standing diamond nanomembranes
    Studies field emission from bulk emitters covered with a thin wide-gap semiconductor layer for mass spectrometry applications. Journal Paper
  • Low-temperature enhancement of the thermoelectric Seebeck coefficient in gated 2D semiconductor nanomembranes
    Formulates a novel controlled scattering approach by simulating a confined 3D material to mimic a 2D material with a gate. Journal Paper
